# ☕ Coffee Sales Analytics | MySQL

### 📊 Project Overview

Turned raw coffee shop transaction data into **actionable business insights using MySQL**. This project explores **revenue performance, product demand, customer purchasing patterns, payment behavior, and time-based sales trends** to understand what drives coffee shop sales.

The analysis progresses from **foundational SQL queries to advanced analytical techniques**, demonstrating practical SQL skills for real-world business analytics.

### 🔍 Key Analysis

📈 **Revenue & Sales Performance**

* Calculated total revenue and overall order volume.
* Analyzed daily and monthly revenue trends.
* Identified the highest-revenue dates and peak sales periods.
* Created cumulative revenue tracking to monitor business growth over time.

☕ **Product Performance**

* Identified the most frequently ordered coffee types.
* Compared revenue contribution across different coffee products.
* Ranked the **Top 2 revenue-generating coffees for each time of day**.

⏰ **Time-Based Analysis**

* Analyzed revenue across **Morning, Afternoon, and Night**.
* Identified the hours generating the highest sales.
* Compared sales performance across different weekdays and months.

💳 **Payment & Business Insights**

* Compared revenue generated through **Cash vs. Card payments**.
* Identified months performing above the average monthly revenue.
* Calculated **Month-over-Month (MoM) revenue growth** to track changing sales performance.

### 🚀 Advanced SQL Analysis

Used advanced SQL techniques to move beyond basic reporting:

* **CTEs** for structured and reusable analysis
* **Window Functions** for running totals and revenue contribution
* **LAG()** for Month-over-Month growth analysis
* **DENSE_RANK()** for product ranking
* **NTILE()** for revenue-based segmentation
* **Moving Averages** for identifying sales trends
* **Date & Time Functions** for temporal analysis
* **Aggregate Functions** for revenue and order analysis

### 🛠️ Tech Stack

**MySQL | SQL | CTEs | Window Functions | Data Aggregation | Date & Time Analysis | Business Analytics**

### 💡 Business Value

The analysis helps answer practical business questions such as:

> **Which products generate the most demand?**
> **When does the shop generate peak revenue?**
> **Which coffee types contribute most to overall sales?**
> **How is monthly revenue changing over time?**
> **Which periods outperform the business's average sales?**

This project demonstrates how **SQL can transform transactional data into meaningful insights that support data-driven business decisions.**
